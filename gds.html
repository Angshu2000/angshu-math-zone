India Post GDS Official Notification 2026

Notification No. 17-12/2026-GDS dated 20 August 2026

📄 Official Notification PDF 📝 Apply / Official Portal

📅 Important Dates

ActivityDate
One-Time Registration31 August 2026 to 19 September 2026 (up to 17:00 hrs)
Online Application & Fee Payment02 September 2026 to 21 September 2026 (up to 17:00 hrs)
Correction / Edit Window23 September 2026 to 24 September 2026 (up to 17:00 hrs)

🎓 Eligibility

AgeMinimum 18 years; maximum 40 years, subject to applicable relaxation.
Education10th standard Secondary School Examination pass with passing marks in Mathematics and English from a recognized Board.
Local LanguageCandidate should have studied the prescribed local language at least up to 10th standard.
SelectionShortlisting is based on a system-generated merit list using 10th-standard marks/converted grades as specified in the notification.
Selection basis: The notification states that the merit list is prepared from Secondary School Examination (10th standard) marks, aggregated to percentage with accuracy of 4 decimals.

💰 TRCA / Emoluments

BPMRs. 12,000 to Rs. 29,380
ABPM / Dak SevakRs. 10,000 to Rs. 24,470

The notification describes these as Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA) slabs, with applicable DA and other benefits under the GDS rules.

💳 Application Fee

  • Rs. 100 for applicants for all posts notified in the choice of Division.
  • Fee is exempted for all female applicants, SC/ST applicants, PwBD and Transwomen applicants.
  • For non-exempt applicants, online payment modes including recognized Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking and UPI are available as specified.

📊 Vacancy Details

The official notification provides the vacancy position division-wise in Annexure-I; the division-wise vacancy position is made available on the GDS portal.

For the latest circle/division-wise vacancy, always check the official GDS portal before applying because the Department reserves the right to revise the number of posts.

🧾 Selection & Verification

  • Shortlisted candidates are informed through the GDS Online Engagement Portal and registered SMS/email.
  • Shortlisted candidates must attend physical document verification within the prescribed period.
  • Documents may include marksheet, identity proof, caste/PwBD/EWS/transgender certificates as applicable, date-of-birth proof and medical certificate.
  • After successful verification, a system-generated offer of provisional engagement is issued.
